# Texture credits — HARAQ (حَرَق) v0.4.0

All textures in this folder come from **Poly Haven** (https://polyhaven.com) and are released under
**CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)** — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ .
No attribution is required; credit is given here as thanks. Files were downloaded at 1K, resized to
1024 px (sand) or 512 px (everything else) and, where noted, colour-graded toward the HARAQ palette.
Godot imports them VRAM-compressed (ETC2 on Android, S3TC on desktop) with mipmaps.
